## 🔥 Stuff I've built

**[MedBrief](https://med-brief-one.vercel.app/)** is the one I'd show you first. Lab reports read like ancient scrolls to most people, so I built a live portal where a doctor and patient can chat while the report gets turned into a plain-English summary. Solo, three months, shipped for real.

**Offline Navigation (SIH 2026)** guesses where a vehicle is without any GPS by following its motion and fixing the drift as it goes. Half math, half stubbornness.

**[SkillSync](https://github.com/Jatin-2075)** lets developers prove their skills with a test instead of just claiming them on a profile, and lets recruiters actually find them.

**Black Hole Simulation** is a physics engine I wrote from scratch so I could watch light bend around a black hole. No practical use whatsoever, loved every minute.
